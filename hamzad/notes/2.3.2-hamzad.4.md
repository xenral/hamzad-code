## What's new

- **Persian reads right to left, with joined letters**, in terminals that draw text exactly as written: VS Code, Cursor, iTerm2, Ghostty, WezTerm, kitty, Alacritty, Warp and Windows Terminal. English words, numbers and code inside a Persian sentence keep their own order, brackets included, and the frames and labels around the text stay where they are. Copying still gives the text as it was written.
- **Typing Persian in the prompt** shows it the same way, with the caret where the next letter will appear.
- **`hamzad run`** prints Persian the same way when it writes to a terminal.
- It is on by default where it is needed. Set `"rtl": "on"` or `"off"` in `~/.config/hamzad/cli.json`, or `HAMZAD_RTL=on|off`, to choose; Apple's Terminal is left as it is unless you turn it on.
- **The credits left show within seconds** of opening Hamzad, instead of up to a minute later.
