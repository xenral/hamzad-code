## What's new

- **A new look in the terminal.** The conversation sits in one framed window with the project folder on its top edge; your messages read `you ›` and the answers `hamzad ›`, and each step Hamzad takes (reading, searching, editing, running) is one line.
- **The prompt has its own frame**, named by the mode and the model you are using. Its bottom edge shows how much of the context is used, the credits left and `ctrl+p menu`; while an answer is being written it shows `esc to stop`.
- **"Code" and "Plan" modes.** The mode that writes code is now called Code; `shift+tab` switches mode.
- **Hamzad's own words** in the prompt (`Tell Hamzad what to do…`) and its examples, and a clear "Not signed in · hamzad auth login" when you are signed out.
