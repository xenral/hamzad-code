## What's new

- **Updates install themselves.** A new version is installed in the background when you open Hamzad CLI and is used from the next start. To be asked first, or to switch it off, set `"update": "notify"` or `"update": "disable"` in `~/.config/hamzad/hamzad.json`.
- **`/usage` shows your Hamzad account:** the credits left, what is used but not yet in your credit history, and what each model has used over the last 30 days.
- **The credits left are shown to the hundredth** and refresh by themselves, so you see them move after a prompt.
- **Settings can live in `hamzad.json`** (or `hamzad.jsonc`), in a project or in `~/.config/hamzad`.
- **A model that cannot be served right now is said once**, with a clear message, instead of being tried again and again.
